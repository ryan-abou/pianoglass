# Where each song came from

Every composition here is in the public domain. This records that, and who
made each file.

## Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star (`TwinkleTwinkle.mid`)

Traditional French melody, "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman", 1761. Public domain.
Written for PianoGlass by `Assets/Editor/StarterSongWriter.cs` and released as
CC0.

## Ode to Joy (`OdeToJoy.mid`)

Ludwig van Beethoven, 1824. Public domain. Written for PianoGlass by
`Assets/Editor/StarterSongWriter.cs` and released as CC0.

## Minuet in G major (`MinuetInG.mid`)

Minuet in G major, BWV Anh. 114 (Christian Petzold, c. 1725, public domain).
Source sheet: the PianoXML engraving, marked "Public Domain (PianoXML typeset)"
at the foot of the score, downloaded from
https://en.truongca.com/nhac/7294-bach-minuet-in-g-major-bwv-anh-114 .
Turned into MIDI by Ryan with help from Claude, and checked bar by bar against
that sheet. Repeats are not written out.

The website itself names no licence, only a general "for reference,
non-commercial" note. The sheet's own public-domain label is what counts. A
copy of that PDF is kept at `sources/MinuetInG-PianoXML.pdf`, so the evidence
survives if the site changes.
